Setting Up an iPad

  1. Open this page on the iPad in Safari, make your choices, and tap Open This Sign.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Name it after its spot, like "Forest Door," and tap Add.
  3. Open the sign from its new Home Screen icon. It fills the screen with no Safari bars.
  4. Lock it to the sign with Guided Access: triple-click the Home button (or the top button on iPads without one) and tap Start.
  5. Plug it in. The sign stays current on its own.
